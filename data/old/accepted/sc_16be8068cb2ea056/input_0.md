Jerry E. Smith, Circuit Judge: Sidney Kimble has been convicted of two drug-trafficking felonies. After serving his time for each offense, he was found in possession of a hand- gun and charged and convicted under 18 U.S.C. § 922(g)(1), which bars felons from possessing firearms. His appeal posits that prohibiting him from keeping or bearing arms for the remainder of his life violates the Second Amendment. Because disarming drug traffickers accords with the nation’s history and tradition of firearm regulation, we affirm Kimble’s conviction.
