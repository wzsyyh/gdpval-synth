UNITED STATES COURT OF APPEALS FOR THE SECOND CIRCUIT August Term, 2024 (Argued: May 14, 2025 Decided: July 2, 2025) Docket No. 24-2209-cv MOLECULAR DYNAMICS, LTD., SDBM LIMITED, CHAUNCEY CAPITAL CORP., Petitioners-Appellants, v. SPECTRUM DYNAMICS MEDICAL LIMITED, BIOSENSORS INTERNATIONAL GROUP LTD., Respondents-Appellees. 1 Before: SACK, WESLEY, AND ROBINSON, Circuit Judges. Petitioners-Appellants were on the losing end of an arbitration held in Geneva, Switzerland, which resulted in the award of a substantial monetary sum, declaratory relief, and costs and attorneys’ fees to Respondents-Appellees. In accord with an agreement between the parties that New York courts would have exclusive jurisdiction over all matters concerning the arbitration, Petitioners-Appellants filed a petition to vacate the arbitral awards in the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York. The district court (Katherine P. Failla, Judge) denied the petition, concluding that it lacked subject- matter jurisdiction to vacate the Swiss-made awards under the Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards (the “New York Convention” or the “Convention”) because, in the district court’s view, the awards may only be vacated in the country where they were made, Switzerland. 1The Clerk of Court is respectfully directed to amend the official caption as set forth above.
